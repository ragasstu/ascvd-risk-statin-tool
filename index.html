Diabetes risk enhancement
Diabetes-specific risk enhancers: T2DM ≥10 years, T1DM ≥20 years, albuminuria ≥30 mg/g, eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m², diabetic retinopathy, diabetic neuropathy, or ABI <0.9.

Calculation summary

PREVENT‑ASCVD is a risk-estimation tool, not a substitute for clinical judgment. Risk enhancers, comorbidities, medication tolerance, pregnancy considerations, drug interactions, patient preference, and other guideline pathways may alter management.

Sources & note

External-validity note: PREVENT was developed and validated primarily in U.S. populations. When used outside the United States, including in Saudi Arabia, interpret the estimated risk in clinical context because baseline risk and population characteristics may differ.
